Privacy Policy — TempoLock BPM

Last updated: August 2026

TempoLock BPM ("the app") is developed by BeatsMove LLC. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Microphone Access

The app uses the device microphone to analyze the tempo of music playing nearby. Audio is processed entirely on your device in real time. No audio is ever recorded, stored, or transmitted off your device.

Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. The app stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Tempo log entries (BPM readings and timestamps)
  • Setlists and song tempos you create
  • App preferences and settings

This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to any server, shared with any third party, or used for analytics of any kind.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Deletion

All app data is stored locally on your device. You can delete it at any time by deleting the app.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
